# Business Questions

## Project Goal

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to transform operational data into actionable business intelligence.

---

# Job Order Analysis

## Question 1

Which organizations currently have active job openings?

### Business Value

Provides visibility into employer engagement and current hiring activity.

---

## Question 2

Which locations have the highest concentration of job opportunities?

### Business Value

Supports workforce planning and labor market analysis.

---

## Question 3

What compensation ranges are being offered?

### Business Value

Helps identify wage trends and salary competitiveness.

---

## Question 4

Which occupations are most frequently posted?

### Business Value

Provides insight into labor demand and skill shortages.

---

## Question 5

What application methods are available to candidates?

### Business Value

Evaluates accessibility and candidate engagement channels.

---

## Question 6

Which positions offer benefits?

### Business Value

Highlights employer competitiveness and job quality.

---

## Question 7

Which opportunities participate in special workforce initiatives?

### Business Value

Supports policy and program reporting.
